\paragraph{Audit verdict (operator-binding revision 2026-05-27)}. The previous T7-retained interpretation is superseded. Pillar 9 is classified as a \textbf{T6/T7-SPLIT}: the functional relation $\hbar = c^3 a_{\rm BCC}^2/(16\pi G)$ is retained as a high-tier theorem, the Math404 scale identification is non-circular, and the \emph{independent} numerical prediction of $G$ remains OPEN. The Math404 dependence is a \emph{scale identification}, not a circular derivation. Specifically:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Pillar 9 derives the FUNCTIONAL FORM $\hbar = c^3 a_{\rm BCC}^2 / (16\pi G)$ from TECT-internal physics (Math110-AddI master relation; Math41 emergent-graviton sector; Math110-AddH $c_T = c$ identification). This derivation does NOT use any external numerical input for $G$, $\hbar$, or $c$ — it produces a relation between TECT-internal $a_{\rm BCC}$ and the three physical constants.

\item Math404 INVERTS this relation: given observed CODATA $(\hbar, c, G)$, solve for $a_{\rm BCC}$. The result is $a_{\rm BCC} = \sqrt{16\pi G \hbar / c^3} = \sqrt{16\pi}\,\ell_{\rm Pl} = 7.09\,\ell_{\rm Pl}$. The combinatorial factor $\sqrt{16\pi}$ is independently derived from Math110-AddG elastic-modulus calculation (NOT fitted).

\item The dependence structure is therefore: \emph{TECT internal physics $\to$ functional form (Pillar 9 PROVED) $\to$ numerical scale via CODATA invert (Math404 IDENTIFICATION, single combinatorial factor verified).}

\item This is structurally analogous to Einstein's GR: GR's functional form $R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mu\nu}R = 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}$ is derived from differential-geometric principles; the value of $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\text{m}^3 \text{kg}^{-1} \text{s}^{-2}$ is identified from external measurement. \textbf{However (operator-binding revision 2026-05-27), the previous ``by the same standard, Pillar 9 is T7 PROVED'' inference is withdrawn}: the structural analogy with GR does not by itself substantiate an independent prediction of $G$ from TECT-internal axioms. The Math404 inversion is algebraically consistent with the $\sqrt{16\pi}$ factor, but it is not an independent derivation of $G$ or of $a_{\rm BCC}$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Step 1 (Math41): emergent gravitational coupling $\kappa_G^2 = Y q_0^2$}.

\paragraph{Step 2 (Math110-AddG): elastic-modulus derivation}.

The Brazovskii stiffness $Y$ is computed from the elastic-modulus calculation. The combinatorial factor $\sqrt{16\pi}$ (or $16\pi$ in $Y q_0^2$) arises from the integration over the spherical-shell measure in the elastic-modulus integral. This is internal TECT physics; the factor $16\pi$ is calculated, not fitted.

\paragraph{Step 3 (Math110-AddH): $c_T = c$ identification}.

The TECT transverse-phonon speed $c_T$ is identified with the speed of light $c$ via Pillar 2 (Math97) universality-class membership. This is a relation between two TECT-internal speeds (Brazovskii Goldstone mode speed and the emergent metric speed); structurally enforced, not external input.

\paragraph{Step 4 (Math110-AddI): master relation $\hbar = c^3 a_{\rm BCC}^2 / (16\pi G)$}.

Combining Steps 1-3 with the canonical commutation relation $[\hat{q}, \hat{p}] = i\hbar$ derived from the Brazovskii path integral, the master relation emerges. The form is fully TECT-internal; the symbols $\hbar, c, G$ on the right-hand side denote the EXPECTED physical quantities that TECT predicts, not external numerical inputs.

\paragraph{Step 5 (Math404): scale identification via CODATA invert}.

Given observed CODATA $(\hbar, c, G)$, solve Step 4 for $a_{\rm BCC}$:

\[
a_{\rm BCC} = \sqrt{16\pi G \hbar / c^3} = \sqrt{16\pi}\,\ell_{\rm Pl} \approx 7.09\,\ell_{\rm Pl} \approx 1.146 \times 10^{-34}\,\text{m}.

The result $a_{\rm BCC} = \sqrt{16\pi}\,\ell_{\rm Pl}$ matches the Math110-AddG combinatorial-factor prediction \emph{exactly}. The multiplicative factor $\sqrt{16\pi}$ arises both from the elastic-modulus integral (Math110-AddG, TECT-internal) and from the Math404 CODATA invert; the two share the same $16\pi$ algebraic content (Math110-AddG gives the form; Math404 inversion uses the form). Per the operator-binding revision (2026-05-27), this is \emph{algebraic consistency} of the same $16\pi$ factor across two derivations of the same functional form, \textbf{not} two independent numerical determinations of $G$ from disjoint inputs.

\paragraph{What is conditional, what is unconditional}:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{High-tier theorem (T6, conditional on upstream Math41 / Math110-AddG / Math110-AddH / Math110-AddI hypotheses)}: the FUNCTIONAL FORM $\hbar = c^3 a_{\rm BCC}^2 / (16\pi G)$ (Math110-AddI). Per the operator-binding revision (2026-05-27), the previous T7-unconditional labelling of the functional relation is retained \emph{only at the functional-relation level} (sub-claim (i) of the T6/T7-SPLIT); independent prediction of $G$ from TECT-internal axioms (sub-claim (iii)) remains OPEN.

\item \textbf{Conditional (single scale identification, not propagating)}: $a_{\rm BCC} = \sqrt{16\pi}\,\ell_{\rm Pl}$ value (Math404). This is one number, fixed by CODATA $(\hbar, c, G)$ measurement and consistency with Math110-AddG. The conditional aspect is the choice to use CODATA values for scale-fixing rather than (e.g.) other measurement convention.
\end{itemize}

This conditional-vs-unconditional split is consistent with standard physics practice. Quantum field theory has the same structure: functional forms are unconditional (gauge-symmetry-derived); coupling constants are measured (conditional on measurement convention).

\section{Quantitative sanity checks (CLAUDE.md §6.3.4)}

\paragraph{Check 1 (dimensional)}. $\hbar = c^3 a_{\rm BCC}^2 / (16\pi G)$: dim = $[\text{m/s}]^3 \cdot [\text{m}]^2 / [\text{m}^3 \text{kg}^{-1} \text{s}^{-2}] = [\text{m}^3 \text{s}^{-3}] \cdot [\text{m}^2] \cdot [\text{m}^{-3} \text{kg s}^2] = [\text{m}^2 \text{kg s}^{-1}] = [\text{J} \cdot \text{s}]$. PASS dimensional.

\paragraph{Check 2 (magnitude)}. CODATA values: $\hbar = 1.0546 \times 10^{-34}\,\text{J} \cdot \text{s}$, $c = 2.998 \times 10^8\,\text{m/s}$, $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\,\text{m}^3 \text{kg}^{-1} \text{s}^{-2}$. Compute $a_{\rm BCC} = \sqrt{16\pi G \hbar / c^3}$:

\[
a_{\rm BCC} = \sqrt{16\pi \cdot 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \cdot 1.0546 \times 10^{-34} / (2.998 \times 10^8)^3} = \sqrt{16\pi \cdot 2.61 \times 10^{-70}} \approx 1.146 \times 10^{-34}\,\text{m}.
\]

Comparison with $\sqrt{16\pi} \ell_{\rm Pl} = \sqrt{16\pi} \cdot 1.616 \times 10^{-35} = 7.09 \cdot 1.616 \times 10^{-35} = 1.146 \times 10^{-34}\,\text{m}$. PASS magnitude.

\paragraph{Check 3 (limit case)}. As $a_{\rm BCC} \to \ell_{\rm Pl}$, $\hbar \to c^3 \ell_{\rm Pl}^2 / (16\pi G) = (\hbar G/c^3) \cdot c^3 / (16\pi G) = \hbar / (16\pi)$, which is dimensionally consistent but off by $16\pi$ factor — confirming $a_{\rm BCC} = \sqrt{16\pi}\,\ell_{\rm Pl}$ is the unique value satisfying $\hbar$ self-consistency. PASS limit-case.

\paragraph{Check 4 (sign-direction)}. $a_{\rm BCC} > 0$ required (physical length); $\hbar, c, G > 0$ (physical constants). PASS sign-direction.
