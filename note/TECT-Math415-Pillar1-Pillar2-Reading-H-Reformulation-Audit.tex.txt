\section{Abstract}
==========================================================================================

The 2026-05-11 Reading H binding (Math401) reframed TECT's canonical vacuum from the pre-Math400 ``BCC is the vacuum'' interpretation to the post-Math400-AddE/AddF ``Brazovskii fluctuation-stabilised disordered phase = vacuum; BCC = stable cosmologically-relevant fluctuation channel'' formulation. This note executes a formal audit of how the Pillar 1 (mass $m^*$) and Pillar 2 (Brazovskii fluctuations) hypothesis chains transform under the Reading H reformulation, and certifies that the T6 PROVED CONDITIONAL labels assigned to both pillars in \texttt{Codes/config/pillar\_status.json} remain mathematically valid post-reformulation.

\paragraph{Key audit findings}.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Pillar 1 hypothesis-set transformation is non-disruptive.} The Math66 (Brazovskii uniqueness) hypothesis transforms cleanly: pre-Math401 ``BCC is the unique global minimiser of $\mathcal{F}[\Psi]$'' is replaced by ``BCC is the unique \emph{cosmologically-relevant stable fluctuation channel} of $\mathcal{F}[\Psi]$ within the Brazovskii disordered ensemble vacuum''. The Math82-AddF numerical anchor $m^{*2} = +4.247 \times 10^{-2}$ at canonical $\mu^2 = +5 \times 10^{-3}$ measures the SAME physical quantity (channel curvature at $|q| = q_*$) under both interpretations; the value is invariant.

\item \textbf{Pillar 2 hypothesis-set transformation introduces one new conditional gate.} The Math82 spectral-gap closure $\lambda_{\min}(\hat{\Delta}_{\rm BCC}) > 0$ is preserved as the channel-stability statement under Reading H, but the interpretation is now: ``the BCC channel is dynamically stable as a fluctuation mode of the Reading-H disordered vacuum'' rather than ``the BCC ground state is dynamically stable.'' This requires verification that the spectral-gap calculation is meaningful in the channel-mode framing, not just the ground-state framing. Math400-AddE Path $\alpha$ confirmation at canonical operating point provides this verification: the one-loop self-consistency that establishes Reading H ALSO confirms BCC-channel stability.

\item \textbf{Hypothesis-set size is unchanged}. Pillar 1: 2 hypotheses (Math66 uniqueness + Math1-v2 BCC channel content, both reformulated); Pillar 2: 1 hypothesis (Math82 spectral gap, reformulated as channel-mode statement).

\item \textbf{T6 PROVED CONDITIONAL retained for both pillars}, but with the conditional hypotheses now explicitly cross-referenced to Math401 (Reading H binding) and Math400-AddE/AddF (technical anchor chain).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Net verdict}:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Pillar 1: T6 PROVED CONDITIONAL on } \mathcal{H}_1^{\rm RH} = \{H_{1,1}^{\rm uniq}, H_{1,2}^{\rm channel}\} \text{ (Math66 + Math1-v2 under Reading H)} \\
&\text{Pillar 2: T6 PROVED CONDITIONAL on } \mathcal{H}_2^{\rm RH} = \{H_{2,1}^{\rm gap}\} \text{ (Math82 spectral gap under Reading H)}
\end{aligned}
}
\]

Two T6 $\to$ T7 promotion follow-ups queued (Math415-AddA, Math415-AddB) for promoting $H_{1,1}^{\rm uniq}$ and $H_{2,1}^{\rm gap}$ from conditional to unconditional via textbook-standard regularity arguments.

\paragraph{Hypothesis set $\mathcal{H}_2^{\rm RH}$ (post-Reading H)}:

\begin{itemize}
\item $H_{2,1}^{\rm gap}$ (Math82 reformulated under Reading H): The Hessian of the Brazovskii free-energy functional around the disordered vacuum has positive minimum eigenvalue $\lambda_{\min} > 0$ restricted to the BCC-channel subspace at $|q| = q_*$. Equivalently, the BCC channel is a TRUE-local-min direction of the fluctuation landscape.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Reformulation analysis}. The Math82 spectral-gap calculation reads the Hessian of $\mathcal{F}[\Psi]$ around a background $\Psi_*$. Pre-Math401: $\Psi_* = \Psi_{\rm BCC}$ (BCC condensate). Post-Reading H: $\Psi_* = 0$ (disordered vacuum), with the analysis restricted to the BCC-channel subspace (modes at $|q| = q_*$).

\emph{Critical reformulation step}: under Reading H, the Hessian eigenvalue spectrum decomposes as

\[
\sigma(\hat{\Delta}_{\rm full}) = \bigsqcup_{n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots} \sigma_n,
\]

where $\sigma_n$ is the spectrum on shell $n$ ($|q| = q_n$). The Math82 calculation projects onto $\sigma_1$ (BCC channel at $|q| = q_*$). The spectral gap is

\[
\lambda_{\min}^{\rm BCC-channel} = \min_{\psi \in \mathrm{span}(\sigma_1)} \langle \psi, \hat{\Delta} \psi \rangle / \langle \psi, \psi \rangle = +r_R \cdot (\text{shell-1 mode coupling}) = +0.4193 \cdot \Theta_1 > 0,
\]

where $\Theta_1$ is the shell-1 mode-coupling factor (positive by Math400-AddF channel-stability check; $n_{\rm neg}^{\rm shell-1} = 0$).

Therefore $H_{2,1}^{\rm gap}$ holds under Reading H \emph{by direct construction from} Math400-AddE Path $\alpha$ ($r_R = +0.4193 > 0$) and Math400-AddF channel-stability ($n_{\rm neg}^{\rm shell-1} = 0$). The Pillar 2 conditional hypothesis is now \emph{automatically satisfied} as a corollary of Reading H, eliminating the need for an independent Math82-only verification under the new framework.

\paragraph{Promotion path to T7 unconditional}. With $H_{2,1}^{\rm gap}$ now derivable from Math400-AddE + Math400-AddF, Pillar 2's T6 conditional could be promoted to T7 unconditional \emph{provided} that the Math400-AddE Path $\alpha$ + Math400-AddF channel-stability chain is itself textbook-standard. Math400-AddE is one-loop self-consistency (textbook for Brazovskii systems); Math400-AddF is numerical (N=64) verification at a single operating point (NOT textbook-standard alone — requires continuum extrapolation). Promotion requires Math415-AddB (continuum-limit extension of Math400-AddF to $N \to \infty$).
