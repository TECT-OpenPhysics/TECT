\section{Yukawa coupling derivation}
==========================================================================================

\paragraph{Cubic vertex.} The TECT cubic anisotropy coupling at the BCC channel is $\lambda_3^{\rm BCC} = A \sim 0.018\,M_{\rm Pl}^2$ from Math410-AddA (exact-RG fixed point). The dimensionful Yukawa for $A_{1g} \to T_{2g} + T_{2g}$ decay:
\[
y_{\rm inf-matter} \sim \frac{\lambda_3^{\rm BCC}}{8\pi^2 m_{\rm inf}^2} \cdot m_{\rm inf} = \frac{\lambda_3^{\rm BCC}}{8\pi^2 m_{\rm inf}}.
\]
At canonical $\lambda_3^{\rm BCC} = 0.018\,M_{\rm Pl}^2$ and $m_{\rm inf} = 10^{14}$ GeV $= 10^{-5}\,M_{\rm Pl}$:
\[
y_{\rm inf-matter} \sim \frac{0.018\,M_{\rm Pl}^2}{8\pi^2 \cdot 10^{-5}\,M_{\rm Pl}} = \frac{0.018}{8\pi^2 \cdot 10^{-5}}\,M_{\rm Pl} = \frac{2.28 \times 10^{-2}}{10^{-5}} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{3}\,M_{\rm Pl}.
\]

\paragraph{Wait — dimensional issue}. Let me redo with proper conventions. The cubic vertex in the canonical normalisation:
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\rm cubic} = -\frac{\lambda_3}{3!} \phi^3, \quad [\lambda_3] = M_{\rm Pl}.
\]
For the cubic anisotropy operator $\mathcal{O}_{\rm cubic} = A q_*^4 \phi^2_{\rm cubic}$, the canonical $\lambda_3 \sim A^{1/2} q_*^2 / m_{\rm inf}^{1/2}$. Substituting $A = 0.018\,M_{\rm Pl}^2$, $q_* = 1.30\,M_{\rm Pl}$, $m_{\rm inf} = 10^{-5}\,M_{\rm Pl}$:
\[
\lambda_3 \sim \sqrt{0.018} \cdot 1.69 / \sqrt{10^{-5}} \cdot M_{\rm Pl} = 0.134 \cdot 1.69 / 0.0032 \cdot M_{\rm Pl} \approx 71\,M_{\rm Pl}.
\]
Dimensionless Yukawa via $y = \lambda_3 / m_{\rm inf}$:
\[
y_{\rm inf-matter} \sim 71 / 10^{-5} = 7.1 \times 10^{6}.
\]

\paragraph{This is unphysically large}. Let me re-examine. The cubic anisotropy operator coefficient $A = 0.018\,M_{\rm Pl}^2$ has dimensions of $({\rm mass})^2$ multiplied by the dimensionless $\phi^2_{\rm cubic}$ field combination. The actual cubic Yukawa for inflaton-matter is derived from a 3-field vertex with different dimensional structure.
